Privacy policy

Effective 25 October 2026

Statreel is a personal studio tool that publishes short data videos to the Statreel accounts on YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Facebook. It runs on its owner's own computer. It has no other users and no sign-up, and it does not collect information about viewers.

What the app accesses

To publish videos, the owner connects the Statreel accounts to the app through each platform's official sign-in (OAuth). The app receives access tokens that let it upload videos, set their titles, descriptions and tags, and read basic information about those accounts, such as the channel or page ID and the links to published videos.

The app uses only the Statreel accounts that its owner connects. It does not access anyone else's account, messages, contacts or personal data.

YouTube API Services

Statreel uses YouTube API Services to upload videos to the Statreel YouTube channel and read their status. By using Statreel you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Access granted to Statreel can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page.

How data is stored

Access tokens and the records of published videos are stored only on the owner's own computer. They are never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. The app sends data only to the platform it is publishing to (Google/YouTube, TikTok or Meta), and only to publish or check Statreel's own videos.

Data deletion

To remove Statreel's access to an account and delete the data it holds:

  1. Revoke the app's access in the platform's settings: Google account permissions, TikTok (Settings and privacy, then Security, then Apps), or Facebook and Instagram (Settings, then Apps and websites).
  2. Email CHANNEL_EMAIL to ask for any stored tokens or records to be deleted. They are deleted within 30 days, and we reply to confirm.

Children

Statreel is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new effective date.
