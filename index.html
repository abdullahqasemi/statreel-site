The world, in numbers, in under a minute.

Statreel makes short videos that turn official statistics into surprising stories: who exports the most, which economies are catching up, how countries and companies compare.

Where the numbers come from

Every figure comes from a public, official source: the World Bank, the IMF, the OECD, national statistics offices, and companies' own filings with the US Securities and Exchange Commission. Each video's description lists its sources. We never estimate or fill gaps; a missing value is left out.

How the videos are made

Statreel is a small studio tool that runs on its owner's own computer. It gathers the data, drafts a script with AI assistance, adds a synthetic narration, and renders the charts. A person reviews and approves every video before it is published.

Where to watch

Statreel publishes only to its own accounts on YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Facebook. The app does not post for anyone else and has no other users.
